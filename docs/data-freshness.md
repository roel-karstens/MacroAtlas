# Data freshness

This edition is a dated snapshot. Do not describe a figure as current unless the sentence names the month or the file date.

- Eurostat food in `data/food/eurostat-ea-food.json` was updated 4 November 2026. The last food month is September 2026 (index 143.22, annual rate 1.1). All-items annual rate continues to October 2026 at 4.0. Say “September 2026”, not “now”.
- FAOSTAT production bulk is dated 23 December 2025. Trade bulk is dated 23 July 2026. Both were retrieved 7 November 2026. The latest complete year for wheat, maize, sunflower seed, and export quantity is 2024. Crude sunflower oil production stops at 2023.
- Shares are of the sum of reported country figures. They can include re-exports. They are not unique world trade, bilateral flows, or import dependency. Missing is not zero.
- Ukraine’s 2021 and 2023/2024 figures are not a like-for-like territory. Do not state that occupied areas were excluded unless a later file says so.
- The FAO Food Price Index stays an eight-month table. Do not restore the monthly file or replot it.
- Do not restore Pink Sheet Brent or the ECB 10-year series.
- When a number on the homepage changes, change the sentence in the same commit. Do not bump an article `updated` date if the prose and figures are unchanged.
- Black Sea context uses the United Nations pages retrieved 7 November 2026: Joint Coordination Centre established 27 July 2022; Initiative not renewed after 17 July 2023; over 32 million metric tons to 45 countries while it ran. Do not describe shipping in 2026.
